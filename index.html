About July AI

July AI is building a data marketplace that is focused on serving the needs of humans. Our mission is to increase economic opportunities in the age of AI. We are based in San Francisco, backed by top-tier investors like Basis Set, Night Capital, SV Angel, and Olive Capital.

About the Role

We’re looking for writers, storytellers and creative thinkers to help make a frontier AI model safer.

As an AI red teamer, you’ll write prompts and scenarios designed to trick, confuse, or jailbreak AI models into giving unsafe or unintended responses. Your prompts will be used to improve the safety of a frontier research lab’s newest AI model. This is an opportunity to pivot into the AI field and make real-world impact by helping build the safety infrastructure for a leading AI model, creating safer technology for society as a whole.

New to red teaming? Watch the explainer video (opens in a new tab).

Who We Want

Ideal backgrounds include, but aren’t limited to:

  • Creative writer
  • Screenwriter
  • Cybersecurity / Red teamer
  • Prompt engineer
  • Journalist / Editor
  • Copywriter
  • And other talented creatives who enjoy pushing the limits of AI and have the potential to create high-quality stories and prompts

Bonus skills

  • Ability to think critically, craft storylines and characters, and effectively understand and communicate with AI models in written form
  • Interest in AI safety and ethics

Core Responsibilities

  • Write adversarial prompts and stories that stress-test the AI model, and interpret how the model responds
  • Follow each attack’s criteria and any safety or content rules we provide
  • Document every finding: steps to reproduce, severity, and ideas for a fix
  • Follow NDAs, ethics and safety guidelines

Requirements

  • Submit at least 3 sample red team conversations on our platform (instructions are in the application).
    • Multiple attempts on the same challenge are fine.
  • New to red teaming? We highly recommend the “Red Team - Beginner” module first. You don’t need to finish every challenge, and it greatly improves your chances of being accepted.
