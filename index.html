About July AI

July AI is building a data marketplace that is focused on serving the needs of humans. Our mission is to increase economic opportunities in the age of AI. We are based in San Francisco, backed by top-tier investors like Basis Set, Night Capital, SV Angel, and Olive Capital.

About the Role

We’re looking for writers, storytellers and creative thinkers to help make a frontier AI model safer.

As an AI red teamer, you’ll write prompts and scenarios designed to trick, confuse or jailbreak an AI model into giving unsafe or unintended responses. Your prompts will be used to improve the safety of a frontier research lab’s newest AI model. This is an opportunity to pivot into the AI field and make real-world impact by helping build the safety infrastructure for a leading AI model, creating safer technology for society as a whole.

We’re onboarding contributors now.

New to red teaming? Watch the explainer video (opens in a new tab).

Core responsibilities

  • Craft adversarial test prompts and stories that stress-test the AI model
  • Interpret and understand AI model behavior

Who We Want

Ideal backgrounds include, but aren’t limited to:

  • Creative writer
  • Screenwriter
  • Cybersecurity / Red teamer
  • Prompt engineer
  • Journalist / Editor
  • Copywriter
  • Designer
  • Marketer
  • And other talented creatives who enjoy pushing the limits of AI and have the potential to create high-quality stories and prompts

Bonus skills

  • Ability to think critically, craft storylines and characters, and effectively understand and communicate with AI models in written form
  • Interest in AI safety and ethics
  • Keen to acquire skills in AI

Details

  • Contract position with possibility for full-time
  • Part-time or full-time, 15–40 hours per week
  • SF in-office or remote (must be a US citizen)
  • $30–$70 per hour, open for discussion
  • Direct touchpoints with AI researchers
